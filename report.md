This year, the Hackathon gives everyone an opportunity to solve a meaningful problem and building something together.


## The challenge
How can Bonarda Works use technology to make working with employees, contractors and freelancers easier, more equitable and more effective?


## About Bonarda Works
Bonarda Works is a fictional, growing professional-services company based in Accra.

The company delivers projects across Africa and Europe using a mix of full-time employees, contractors and freelancers working across different locations.

As the company grows, it is becoming more difficult to:

1. Find suitable talent
2. Verify people’s skills
3. Match people to the right work
4. Onboard people efficiently
5. Coordinate work across distributed teams
6. Track work and delivery
7. Maintain an equitable experience for different types of workers
8. Support learning and development
9. Manage payments, rewards and recognition
10. Maintain relationships with people after projects end

Bonarda Works wants to explore how technology could improve one or more of these experiences.


## What your team must do
**Your team must:**

1. Select **one specific problem** within the employee or flexible-worker lifecycle.
2. Identify the person or group most affected by that problem.
3. Understand and clearly explain their needs.
4. Build a focused digital solution that addresses the selected problem.
5. Demonstrate how the solution could create value for Bonarda Works and its workforce.

You are not expected to solve the entire future of work. Select one lifecycle problem, identify the people affected and build a focused solution that addresses it well.

## The employee and flexible-worker lifecycle
**Your team may choose a problem from areas such as:**

1. Discovering opportunities or finding talent
2. Verifying skills and experience
3. Matching people to suitable work
4. Onboarding employees, contractors or freelancers
5. Assigning and coordinating work
6. Supporting distributed collaboration
7. Tracking work and delivery
8. Managing payments, rewards or recognition
9. Supporting learning and development
10. Offboarding and re-engaging people

These are problem areas not prescribed solutions.
